Research & Valuation Methodology Dossier

Mathematical foundations powering the 0 to 100 valuation barometer.

The 0 to 100 Valuation Scale

Every parameter is normalized on a strict 0 to 100 scale:

  • 70 to 100: Undervalued / Good to Buy. High margin of safety.
  • 50 to 69: Fair Value / Neutral. Systematic accumulation.
  • 35 to 49: Moderately Elevated / Hold. Selective trimming.
  • 0 to 34: Severe Froth / Bad to Buy. Rebalance out into safer assets.

Bond-Equity Yield Gap & ERP

Yield Gap compares equity earnings yield against sovereign borrowing rates:

Yield Gap = (100 / Index P/E) - India 10Y G-Sec Yield

When the gap is above -2.50%, equity retains attractive risk premium over risk-free government bonds.

Buffett Indicator (Market Cap / GDP)

The ratio of total listed equities market capitalization to India's nominal GDP. Historical fair-value baseline for India stands between 85% to 100%. Above 115% flags elevated froth in broader market tiers.

Gold / Silver Ratio (GSR)

Gold / Silver Ratio measures how many ounces (or grams) of silver buy one unit of gold. A ratio above 75 indicates Silver is deeply undervalued relative to Gold, creating asymmetric accumulation upside.
